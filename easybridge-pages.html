FAQ

Pricing Questions Answered

Is there a free trial available? ▾
We offer a free 30-minute consultation call for all business services. For training programmes, we provide a sample module preview before enrolment.
Are EMI or instalment options available? ▾
Yes. For courses above ₹5,000 and implementation projects, we offer 2–3 instalment options. Contact us to discuss a plan that works for you.
What's included in the 90-day post-implementation support? ▾
Email and WhatsApp support for any system-related queries, minor configuration adjustments, and a 30-day and 90-day check-in call with your implementation team.
Do you offer GST invoices for all services? ▾
Absolutely. All services are invoiced with a valid GST number. Input tax credit is available for business clients where applicable.
Can I combine multiple services? ▾
Yes — bundling is encouraged. Clients who take 2 or more services receive a 10–15% package discount. Contact us for a custom bundle quote.

The Person Behind EasyBridge

I'm a practicing Chartered Accountant working at the intersection of finance, technology, and business advisory. Over years of working with SMEs across India, I noticed two recurring problems: talented people being turned away for lacking the "right degree" — and businesses struggling to find skilled people or implement the right tools.

EasyBridge was built to solve both. We train the skilled. We implement the tools. We connect businesses with right-fit talent. And we bring CFO-level financial clarity to SMEs who need it without a CFO-sized budget.

My core belief: a skilled person is valued everywhere. Age doesn't matter. A degree is not a prerequisite for growth. What matters is what you can actually do.

Philosophy

What We Believe

Three principles that guide every service EasyBridge offers.

🎓

Skills Over Degrees

A certificate from a university does not guarantee competence. Practical, demonstrated skills do. We train and evaluate on what people can actually do.

🌱

Growth Has No Age

A 50-year-old learning cloud accounting and a 20-year-old starting their career both deserve the same quality of guidance, tools, and opportunity.

🤖

AI Wisely, Not Blindly

Automation should amplify human judgment — not replace it. Especially in finance and compliance, the human CA's role remains irreplaceable.

Our Journey

How EasyBridge Grew

From a solo CA practice to a multi-service advisory bridging skills, technology, and business growth.

2022

Started as a CA practice

Compliance, tax filing, and financial advisory for small businesses in India. Identified the gap between what businesses needed and what available talent could deliver.

2023

Launched cloud training & implementation

Began training students and professionals on Zoho and cloud tools. Implemented Zoho Books and One for first SME clients across Jharkhand and Odisha.

2024

Added AI tools training & FP&A services

Introduced AI tools workshops for CAs and tax professionals — the first India-specific curriculum combining AI with GST, TDS, and litigation workflows.

2025

Launched skill-first recruitment

Noticed businesses hiring by degree rather than capability. Built a recruitment service that screens on skills first — filling finance and operations roles for SMEs.

2026

EasyBridge — the full platform

Five services, one brand. Training, implementation, recruitment, FP&A, and AI upskilling — all under EasyBridge's core belief: skills over degrees, growth at any age.
